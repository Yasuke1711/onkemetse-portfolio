# Little Acacia Guest House

A premium, multi-page hospitality website concept for the Onkemetse portfolio.

## Direction
A private Southern African house shaped by shade, stillness and considered hospitality. The visual system draws on the cinematic scale of Atlantis The Royal, the quiet typographic discipline of Four Seasons and the heritage-led storytelling of Hôtel de Paris, translated into a distinct botanical identity.

## Pages
- Home
- The House
- Stay
- Dining
- Experiences
- Availability
- Enquiry preparation

## Interaction
The date and guest selector filters the illustrative room options. Selecting a room carries the stay details into an enquiry form. The form prepares a message that can be copied locally. No customer data is sent or stored, and there is no live booking inventory, payment or reservation confirmation.

## Concept boundaries
Little Acacia's final country and precise setting have not been selected. The copy therefore uses a broad Southern African identity without inventing an address, local partnerships or destination claims. Property details, accommodation, amenities, rates and availability are illustrative.

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the directory with any static file server.
