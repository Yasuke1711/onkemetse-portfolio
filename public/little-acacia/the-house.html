A SENSE OF BELONGING

Designed for the
way you want to feel.

The acacia tree offers a simple idea: generous shade, a strong centre and branches that make room for others. Little Acacia takes that idea into its architecture, hospitality and everyday rituals.

THE MATERIALS

Honest textures.
Soft light.

Stone, timber, woven fibres and warm neutral tones form a calm backdrop. The spaces are meant to feel collected rather than decorated, comfortable rather than formal.

Openings frame the garden. Shaded outdoor areas extend the day. Every room has a reason to be where it is.
